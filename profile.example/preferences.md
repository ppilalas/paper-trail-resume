# Personal editorial preferences (example)

The agent applies every rule here to every resume, on top of the general rules
in `AGENTS.md`, unless the candidate explicitly reverses one. Keep your real
preferences in `profile/preferences.md`; they never reach the public repo.

Typical things to record here:

## Voice

- Profile voice: first person, plain language, one memorable point of view.
  Vary sentence openings; do not start every sentence with `I`.
- Personality the resume should keep: direct, curious, practical.

## Content rules

- Never state or quantify total years of experience.
- Omit the 2010-2012 retail job; it is not relevant to marketing roles.
- Keep the Contoso pipeline figure attached to "first two quarters"; never
  present it as an annual number.

## Proof metrics

- Preferred highlight metrics, in order: `$1.2M` sourced pipeline (Contoso),
  `3x` demo requests (Fabrikam), `18%` lower CAC (Fabrikam).

## Outreach

- After each resume, draft a short LinkedIn DM to the recruiter or hiring
  manager, starting with `Hey [Name],` when no name is known.
